As of late October 2026, Meta's Muse AI is available in the United States, Canada, Japan and Singapore. Everywhere else it is not available yet, and Meta has not given dates for other countries. This guide lists what is confirmed, what is only rumoured, and what you can do while you wait.

Availability changes quickly, so treat this as a snapshot and check Meta's pages for the latest list.

## Where Muse AI is available

These dates come from Meta announcements as reported by third-party trackers that were last checked on October 29 to 31, 2026:

- **United States:** launched September 8, 2026.
- **Canada:** launched September 18, 2026.
- **Japan:** launched October 23, 2026, the first region outside North America.
- **Singapore:** launched around October 27 to 28, 2026, depending on the time zone.

Mexico was mentioned in some Meta posts and coverage, but no Meta page confirms a launch date. Australia, the UK, the EU, India and other countries have no announced date.

## What Meta says about regions

Meta's subscription help page says Muse is in limited testing and is not available in all locations. To use it you must live in a country where Muse is available, be 18 or the age of majority in your country, and sign in with a Meta account.

Third-party guides add that access follows the region of your Meta account and not only your physical location. Meta has not published exactly how it checks this, so do not assume that a different payment method or device changes your access.

## Is there a waitlist?

One availability tracker states that there is no public waitlist for people in unsupported countries. Meta describes an early-access programme, but it covers new features for people who are already in a supported country. Some guides mention a waitlist screen, so if you see one in the app, follow its instructions.

## What about using a VPN?

You will find guides that explain how to sign up through a VPN. We do not recommend it. Third-party summaries of Meta's terms say that misrepresenting your region is prohibited and can lead to restricted access or a closed account. Your Meta account is more valuable than early access to one app, so the safe choice is to wait for an official launch in your country.

## What to do while you wait

1. **Check the Muse app or muse.ai** from time to time. If your country launches, the sign-in screen will let you in.
2. **Follow Meta's newsroom** for your region, because launch posts appear there first.
3. **Try other assistants in the meantime.** Our comparison of [Muse AI, ChatGPT and Gemini](/blog/muse-ai-vs-chatgpt-vs-gemini) shows where each one is strong.
4. **Do not pay for access you cannot use.** Buying tokens does not unlock Muse in an unsupported country, because access depends on your account and region.

Once you are in a supported country and have a Muse account, you can add tokens on our [Muse AI tokens page](/muse-ai-tokens) or buy them from Meta directly.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which countries can use Muse AI?

The United States, Canada, Japan and Singapore, according to launch announcements reported up to the end of October 2026. No other country has an announced date.

### Is Muse AI available in India?

Not as of late October 2026. India has no announced launch date from Meta.

### Is Muse AI available in the UK or Europe?

Not yet. Meta says it is working to bring Muse to more markets carefully, but it has not given timing.

### Will buying Muse tokens unlock Muse in my country?

No. Access is tied to your Meta account and region. Tokens only add usage for an account that already has access.

### Do I need an invite code to use Muse?

Signing up does not require one. If you enter a friend's invite code within 48 hours of signing up, you and your friend each get a token bonus. Read more in our guide to [Muse AI invite codes](/blog/muse-ai-invite-codes-explained).
