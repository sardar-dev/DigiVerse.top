Muse AI is free to use, but with a weekly usage limit. Reviewers report that the free allowance is about 100 million tokens a week, and for most people that lasts the whole week. Heavy or automated use can run out much sooner.

One important caveat: Meta's own help page says Muse has a free limit but does not state the amount. The 100 million figure comes from launch coverage, and sources disagree on whether Meta has officially confirmed it.

## What is a Muse token?

Every task Muse does uses tokens, which are small chunks of text and work. Meta does not publish what one token buys, so you cannot convert tokens directly into messages or tasks. That is why people judge the limit by what they see in practice.

## What tasks cost, as reported

These figures come from reviewers and users, not from Meta, so treat them as rough examples:

- Making drawings, small games, music files or animated websites used about 6 percent of one free week in one reviewer's test.
- A bigger project, such as building a fake desktop with working apps, used about 11 percent.
- One Reddit user reported that two weeks of regular daily use took about 35 percent of a weekly allowance.
- Another user running a job scan every few hours reported about 31 percent used partway through a week.

The pattern is clear. One-off projects are cheap. Recurring background jobs are what drain the allowance.

## When does the limit reset?

The reset looks weekly and appears to follow the date you signed up. One reviewer who joined on September 8 saw a reset on September 15. Meta has not published the rule, so treat this as unofficial.

## How to get more tokens

1. **Wait for the reset.** If you rarely hit the limit, this costs nothing.
2. **Use an invite code.** Entering a friend's invite code within 48 hours of signing up gives both of you a bonus. Our guide to [Muse AI invite codes](/blog/muse-ai-invite-codes-explained) explains the details and the limits.
3. **Upgrade to a plan.** Meta's Power plan costs $20 per month with 500 million tokens a week, and Maximum costs $100 per month with 3 billion a week. See [Power vs Maximum](/blog/muse-ai-power-vs-maximum).
4. **Buy additional tokens.** Meta lets you buy extra tokens after you reach your weekly limit, with or without a subscription. Tokens bought from Meta do not expire. Independent sellers also offer top-ups, and you can see our current options on the [Muse AI tokens page](/muse-ai-tokens).

## Tips to make the free limit last

- Do one-off projects in one go instead of running them repeatedly.
- Avoid recurring background jobs unless you really need them.
- Check how much of the week you have used before you start a large task.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many free tokens does Muse AI give per week?

Reviewers report about 100 million tokens a week. Meta's help page confirms a free limit but does not state the amount.

### Do unused Muse tokens roll over?

Meta's help page says unused subscription allowance does not roll over to the next week. Tokens you buy separately do not expire.

### Can I use Muse AI without paying?

Yes. Many basic features are free with the weekly limit.

### Is the free limit the same in every country?

Muse is only available in a few countries so far. Check the app for the limit that applies to your account. See [which countries can use Muse AI](/blog/is-muse-ai-available-in-your-country).

### Is it worth buying extra tokens?

Only if you keep reaching your weekly limit and the work is important to you. If you rarely run out, the free tier is enough.
