Meta sells Muse AI in two paid plans. **Power** costs $20 per month and gives 500 million tokens a week. **Maximum** costs $100 per month and gives 3 billion tokens a week. Most people should stay on the free tier first and upgrade only after they hit the limit more than once.

## Power vs Maximum at a glance

- **Power:** $20 per month, 500 million tokens per week, 10 phone calls per day.
- **Maximum:** $100 per month, 3 billion tokens per week, 20 phone calls per day.
- **Free:** a weekly limit that Meta does not state. Reviewers report about 100 million tokens a week.

These numbers come from Meta's Help Center. Prices are in US dollars and differ by country. In Japan, for example, the app showed 3,499 yen per month for Power and 16,990 yen for Maximum when checked on October 26, 2026.

## Which plan costs less per token?

Weekly allowance does not roll over, so a simple monthly comparison uses four weeks:

- **Power:** 500 million times 4 is about 2 billion tokens a month for $20, or roughly $10 per billion tokens.
- **Maximum:** 3 billion times 4 is about 12 billion tokens a month for $100, or roughly $8.30 per billion tokens.

These are rough figures that only count tokens you actually use. If you leave part of the allowance unused, the real cost per token you use is higher.

## Who should choose what

**Stay on the free tier** if you use Muse for occasional projects. Reviewers report that most people do not run out of the free allowance.

**Choose Power** if you hit the free limit every week and you use Muse for regular work, such as research, coding help or small automations.

**Choose Maximum** if Muse is part of your daily workflow, you run many background tasks, or you share heavy work across a day.

## A third option: buy extra tokens

Meta also lets you buy additional tokens once you reach your weekly limit, with or without a subscription. Purchased tokens do not expire and stay available if you cancel a plan. This suits people who need a one-off boost, such as a big project week, and do not want a monthly bill.

Independent sellers also offer top-ups to your own account. If you want to compare, see the options on the [Muse AI tokens page](/muse-ai-tokens). If Meta's own checkout works for you, that is the official route.

## Things to check before you pay

1. **Availability.** Muse works only in some countries. Check [where Muse AI is available](/is-muse-ai-available-in-your-country) before you pay.
2. **Renewal.** Plans renew monthly until you cancel, on the same date as your first purchase.
3. **Other tools.** If neither plan fits, see [Meta Muse AI alternatives](/meta-muse-ai-alternatives) before you commit.
4. **Your real usage.** Look at how much of your weekly allowance you use. Our guide to the [Muse free limit](/muse-ai-free-limit-how-many-tokens) shows what tasks cost.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does Muse AI cost?

Muse is free with a weekly limit. Power costs $20 per month and Maximum costs $100 per month in the US, according to Meta's Help Center.

### How many tokens does Muse Power include?

500 million tokens per week.

### How many tokens does Muse Maximum include?

3 billion tokens per week.

### Do unused tokens carry over to the next week?

Meta says unused subscription allowance does not roll over. Tokens you buy separately do not expire.

### Can I buy extra tokens without a subscription?

Yes. Meta lets you buy additional tokens after you reach your weekly limit, with or without a subscription.
