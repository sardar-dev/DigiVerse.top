## Which "Muse" do you mean?

The word "Muse" is used for a few different products, and this causes a lot of wrong clicks:

- **Meta Muse (the agent).** Meta's personal AI agent that launched on September 8, 2026. It can help with email, forms, travel and shopping, and it asks for approval before sensitive steps. This is the main topic of this guide.
- **Muse Image.** Meta's image model, reported to have launched in July 2026. It powers image tools in Meta AI. We cover it in its own section below.
- **muse.ai.** A video hosting platform. It has nothing to do with Meta.
- **Sudowrite Muse.** An AI model for fiction writers, also unrelated.

If you wanted one of the last two, this article is not the right one. Searching for the product name plus "alternatives" on a software review site will help you more.

## Why people look for a Meta Muse alternative

Based on reviews written since launch, these are the most common reasons:

1. **It is not available where they live.** Muse started in the US. Canada followed, and Japan and Singapore came later. Our guide on [which countries can use Muse AI](/blog/is-muse-ai-available-in-your-country) has the latest list.
2. **They hit the usage limit.** The free tier has a weekly limit. See how much you really get in our [free limit guide](/blog/muse-ai-free-limit-how-many-tokens).
3. **They worry about privacy.** Meta's record makes some people nervous. One review also says memory is on by default. Meta has disputed some of the claims people have made about data access, so read its settings yourself.
4. **It asks before it acts.** The approval step is good for safety, but some people find it slow.
5. **It makes mistakes.** Early reviewers reported wrong details and places that had closed. Every AI agent does this sometimes, so always check anything important.
6. **It runs only in Meta's cloud.** You cannot host it yourself, and it cannot work with files on your own computer.

None of these is a reason to panic. They are reasons to pick the right tool for your own situation.

## The 9 alternatives

Prices below are the ones reported by reviewers between September and November 2026. They change often, so check the official page before you pay.

### 1. ChatGPT

**Best for:** people who want the closest all-round replacement.

ChatGPT is the tool most people already know. It writes, researches and answers questions well, and its agent mode can use a virtual browser to do tasks for you. Reviewers list Plus at about $20 per month. Agent mode is paid only.

**Watch out for:** agent use is metered, and OpenAI does not publish an exact allowance. Also, ChatGPT agent mode is not the same as a Muse-style agent that works inside your accounts, so test it on a real task first.

Want a deeper side-by-side? Read our [Muse AI vs ChatGPT vs Gemini](/blog/muse-ai-vs-chatgpt-vs-gemini) comparison.

### 2. Google Gemini

**Best for:** people who already use Gmail, Google Docs, Drive and Calendar.

Gemini fits naturally into Google apps. It has a Deep Research mode for long reports and an agent mode on higher plans. Reviewers list AI Pro at about $19.99 per month, with a cheaper AI Plus plan at $4.99.

**Watch out for:** the best agent features are on the expensive tiers, and the usage limits are described vaguely.

### 3. Claude

**Best for:** people who want a careful assistant that stops and asks before doing something risky.

Claude has a browser extension for Chrome that works on web tasks, and it is known for being cautious with sensitive actions. Reviewers list Pro at about $20 per month, or about $17 per month when billed yearly. Heavier use needs Max, which starts around $100.

**Watch out for:** shared usage limits can run out faster than you expect during long tasks.

### 4. Perplexity Comet

**Best for:** research where you want sources next to every answer.

Comet is an AI browser. It mixes search with the ability to act on pages. Pro is reported at about $20 per month.

**Watch out for:** it is built for research, not for managing your personal life. It has no strong memory of you the way a personal agent does.

### 5. Manus

**Best for:** getting a finished report, website or slide deck from one request.

Manus works on its own and hands back a finished file. It has a free tier with daily credits, and paid plans reported from about $20 per month.

**Watch out for:** credit costs can be hard to predict on big tasks. Set a small test job first.

### 6. Carly

**Best for:** work email, calendars and business apps.

Carly is built around the working day. It can handle several Google or Microsoft accounts and start tasks when something happens, such as a new email. Its AI agents are reported from about $35 per month, and it has a free workflow tier.

**Watch out for:** the details above come from Carly's own site, so treat them as vendor claims. Work accounts may also need an admin to approve access.

### 7. Vellum

**Best for:** people who want control over their data and model choice.

Vellum is open source under the MIT license. You can run it on your own machine or in the cloud, and you can choose which AI model it uses. Self-hosting is free. Cloud plans are reported from about $30 per month.

**Watch out for:** setup is more technical than a normal app, and the review that praises it was written by Vellum itself.

### 8. Jan

**Best for:** fully offline chat on your own computer.

Jan runs open models locally and is free. Nothing leaves your machine, which is the strongest privacy you can get.

**Watch out for:** it is a chat tool, not an agent. It will not book, buy or browse for you, and the quality depends on your computer and the model you pick.

### 9. Poke

**Best for:** people who prefer to manage tasks by text message.

Poke is an assistant you talk to through messages, and it connects to Gmail or Outlook. Reviewers list a free plan and a Pro plan at about $19 per month.

**Watch out for:** it is a newer product, and reviewers note that its company has changed hands, so the roadmap may shift.

## If you meant Muse Image

Some people search for a Muse alternative because they want to make pictures, not run an agent. These tools get the most mentions in recent comparisons:

- **Gemini (Nano Banana):** the easiest free place to start.
- **Midjourney:** polished, artistic style. Plans start around $10 per month, and there is no free tier.
- **Ideogram:** good when you need readable text inside an image. The free tier gives about 30 credits a week.
- **Adobe Firefly:** useful if you care about commercial licensing.
- **Canva AI:** best when the image goes straight into a design.

Meta's own image tools are free for everyday use, so try them first if you only make the odd picture.

## How to choose in three questions

1. **Is Muse available where I live?** If not, start with ChatGPT or Gemini.
2. **What will I use it for most?** Email and calendar: Gemini or Carly. Research: Perplexity Comet. Finished documents: Manus. General help: ChatGPT.
3. **How private does it need to be?** If the answer is "very", look at Vellum or Jan.

Then test your top two for one week on a real task from your day. Do not judge a tool by a single demo.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Trusting every "best of" list.** Many of them are written by companies that rank their own product first. That includes the sources below and us.
- **Giving an agent your passwords.** Use proper sign-in or app connections, and keep approval prompts switched on.
- **Paying yearly before testing.** Start monthly and cancel if it does not fit.
- **Using a VPN to get around country limits.** It can break the terms of the service and put your account at risk.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best Meta Muse alternative?

For most people it is ChatGPT, because it is the closest all-round tool and is available in many countries. If you use Google apps all day, Gemini may suit you better.

### Is there a free Meta Muse alternative?

Yes. ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity all have free tiers, and Jan is fully free. The agent features that act for you are usually paid, so free tiers are mainly good for chat and research.

### What can I use if Meta Muse is not available in my country?

ChatGPT and Gemini are available in many more countries. Check the official page for your country before you pay, because rules change.

### Which Muse alternative is the most private?

Jan is the most private because it runs on your own computer. Vellum is also a strong choice, because you can host it yourself and pick the model.

### What is the best alternative to Muse Image?

For free use, start with Gemini. For artistic images, try Midjourney. If you need clear text in your pictures, try Ideogram.

### Is muse.ai the same as Meta Muse?

No. muse.ai is a video hosting platform and is not connected to Meta.

### Is Meta Muse worth it compared to ChatGPT?

It depends on the job. Muse is free to try and is built for errands like forms and shopping. ChatGPT is the more mature all-rounder. The best test is to give both the same real task and compare the results.
